# HGB-B1 component atlas v1

Mode: built-in `image_gen` with the canonical B1 detailed-design board as the
geometry reference. These are photorealistic concept visualizations, not analysis
models or construction details.

## Common invariant prompt

Preserve HGB-B1 topology: `145+250+650+250+145=1,440 m`; exactly two towers; one
continuous ground-anchored main cable; vertical hangers only in the 650 m main span;
separate stay fans only landward of each tower; no inward stays; exactly one central
under-deck arch between tower feet; exactly two side half-arches from CATB to tower
feet; no mid-river pier; no duplicated arch or floating member.

## Final prompt set

### 01 — Complete bridge

Photorealistic architectural visualization, low aerial three-quarter view, entire
HGB-B1 visible across a wide river. Use pale HPC towers, dark graphite aerodynamic
steel box, terracotta-grey CFST-UHPC arch, galvanized cables, inspection walkways,
realistic weathering and crisp daylight. No text, vehicles, people, fantasy forms,
extra towers, inward stays, decorative truss or mid-river piers.

### 02 — Tower and saddle

Photorealistic close-up of one longitudinally near-vertical tower with subtly inclined
transverse legs. Show the continuous main cable over a replaceable machined saddle,
vertical hangers toward the main span, stay fan only landward, anchor boxes, access,
dehumidification, dampers, drainage, handrails and lightning protection. No labels,
extra cables or sci-fi elements.

### 03 — Aerodynamic deck module

Photorealistic isometric cutaway of a 32 m concept deck module: closed aerodynamic
steel box, internal cells/diaphragms, orthotropic steel plate, replaceable UHPC
cassettes, permanent inclined overhang ribs, two cable planes, inspection corridor,
drainage, cable trays, dehumidification and access. CFRP appears only as localized,
fire-protected fatigue strengthening. No open truss or hydraulic ribs.

### 04 — Arch, tower foot and foundation

Photorealistic structural cutaway of one tower-foot node. One central CFST-UHPC arch
from the river side and one side half-arch from landward enter opposite faces with
continuous tangency. Show steel shell, UHPC infill, ring diaphragms, shear studs,
transition plates, confinement reinforcement, sensors, pile cap, vertical and
battered piles, soil/rock and restrained load-flow arrows. No floating ends or void.

### 05 — Cable and anchor system

Photorealistic component assembly: main-cable compacted galvanized wires, sealed
wrapping/dehumidification; hanger fork/socket/pin; separate parallel-strand landward
stay with HDPE sheath and damper; deck anchor box; CATB chamber with splay saddle,
sockets, plates, access and drainage. Show real replaceable hardware and sensors; do
not merge main cable and stay into one system.
